# Race descriptives: RACECEN1/RACECEN2/RACECEN3

General Social Survey, 2004-2024. Race is asked as a select-all-that-apply question, recorded across up to three variables (first/second/third mention). "Mixed race" below means the respondent's mentions span more than one broad race category (selecting two Asian subgroups, e.g. Chinese and Filipino, still counts as "Single race" here, matching how GSS_happiness_plot.py buckets race).
