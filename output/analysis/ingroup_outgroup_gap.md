# In-group vs. out-group happiness predictions

Forecasted happiness: own subgroup − predicted mean of the other subgroups in the group

`Gap` = `Own-group pred.` − `Mean other-group pred.`, where `Mean other-group pred.` is the average of that subgroup's members' mean predictions for each other subgroup in the group (the average of the per-subgroup averages). Positive = the subgroup rates its own group happier than it rates the rest of the group. `True GSS mean` is the WTSSNRPS-weighted 2004-2024 average, used only to order rows.
